## Featured Product Project

### Customer Insights Intelligence

An end-to-end **GenAI customer-insights product prototype** designed to help Product Managers turn fragmented customer feedback into actionable product intelligence.

The experience combines:

* An insights dashboard that identifies recurring customer problems and surfaces **evidence-backed product opportunities**
* **LLM-powered analysis** to interpret qualitative customer feedback
* A **RAG-based conversational experience** that allows Product Managers to retrieve and explore relevant customer evidence through natural-language questions
* Product and technical evaluation across **customer value, solution quality, grounding, latency, cost, and scalability**

**Product Goal:** Help Product Managers move from scattered customer feedback to evidence-backed product decisions faster.
